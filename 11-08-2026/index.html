Eisenhower Matrix

For those unaware, the Eisenhower Matrix is a table essentially with four squares, 2 by 2. The matrix is divided into four sections, urgent and important, urgent and not important, important and not urgent, and not important and not urgent. Now this is commonly used in to do lists but where it came up for me was in scheduling in a computer system especially those used in aeroplanes. It got me reflecting on what is urgent and not important for me and what is important and not urgent. How could I be better focusing on what is important and not as urgent? So I modified my to do list database to have an Eisenhower mode. Where if I know I have a task coming up that is of great importance and perhaps urgency but hasn't been given the time it deserves being replaced by less important but more urgent tasks in my database, rather than update the importance like I would usually it instead downgrades the urgency of all other task. This was a massive help over the last fortnight and I'm so glad my coursework can inspire me in unexpected ways, so too does my work.
