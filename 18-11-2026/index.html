Additional Events

More often the events supersede the boundaries of a label and hence they arrive below so other events that do not fit this week are:
I went to a Jazz Bar for the first time to watch some live jazz and this was a moving experience. The improvisational aspects and the flow of music was unreal and I really enjoyed the space itself. This was a great use of a night out and I really enjoyed the energy.
I went to the opening weekend of the Powerhouse Parramatta with a friend. This was very cool. The exhibits were extroadinary, very vast like the building itself. Often times it felt like the building itself was a comment on the industrial nature of our age. Overall each of the exhibits were modern and recognisable in our current culture, self reflecting the time we are in now, which is both scary and exciting. A great thing to ponder.
I took some time to attend another talk on Fast Fourier Transforms and after quite a lot of time pondering and trying to understand this concept the more I expose myself to it the closer I get to understanding it. I find this concept really challenging though so I think I should additionally sit with it in the future and take the time to learn it again.
I watched the college play, which was so funny and really came together this year in a way that outshone the last two. This really felt like a play that tried to maximise the experience and was in general very funny all round so great to see.

With the reliance we have on our oceans for food and the economy. These warnings and the guidance should not be overlooked and there was a clear value put onto having sanctuaries for marine life, free of overfishing, which would be a forward step.
I went to a friends exhibition, in particular for their photography. I felt a strong sense of storytelling while looking at the pieces, one of great suffering and pain and anger, which aptly was probably not the intention of the piece but overall was what I took away from the piece. The greatest emotions one can feel as an outcast. The other pieces at the exhibition additionally had some emotional impact one being a piece about the nature of cars in relation to how they have such an ability to hurt animals that I found rather upsetting, yet another mirror reflection to see the world around us.
I went and watched the sports grand final for the college, in which we took the win in soccer. There was such an energy that I am glad I was able to see and experience. It has been a shame I have not been able to attend their weekly games this year because of other commitments but seeing each player find themselves within the match showed that it was a deserving win.
Additionally we had the final formal dinner of the year, which will be my final formal dinner at the accommodation I have spent the last 3 years at. I really found myself reflecting throughout the dinner and I think that overall it has been an amazing opportunity to live where I live and be around so many different people every day in these spaces. I look forward to the change but am really finding I appreciate the time I spent here.
