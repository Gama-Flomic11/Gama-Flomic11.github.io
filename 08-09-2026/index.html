I have been out on a couple 'Study Crawls' throughout the city / university. The term essentially plays on the idea of a Pub Crawl but with study spots, so instead of studying in one spot I get to move around and study. These have been great at improving my mood, getting outdoors and giving my eyes time the rest between locking in. I have taken a few photos along the way one of which is the header photo of this blog. I may get a couple strange looks here and there studying in front of the Museum of Contemporary Art with view of the Opera House on a Sunday afternoon but I really enjoyed it and this next course has been very interesting to get an early start on, so exciting stuff. On this particular crawl I found some very interesting spots that could be used to study around The Rocks and Hyde Park on a sunny day when I can do offline tasks. I stumbled upon a market which was exciting to walk through and a garden type exhibit outside the station for the start of spring. At the start of my crawl at the university I found the exhibit on space in the library and spent a few hours going between studying and looking at the exhibit. I plan to continue this format because it is really interesting to explore and not hard to find a bench or a spot to sit for half an hour to focus before the next movement. The format is a lot more productive at the univeristy though because of the distance between locations.
For a short review of 'Tony', as someone not familiar with Anthony Bourdain, I really enjoyed it. It was smart to only focus on a short period of his life and there was not a moment I was taken out of the film. The characterisation was model of how movies should do characters and the acting only heightened that. The nature of a short period though meant that some plot points that a non-biopic would have used to call back on were not used as call backs, because it is meant to be based on reality, as far as I was understanding the process while in the cinema. To which I am really glad they stuck with because the excessive focus on delivery on every plot point is often tiresome especially when closing out a movie or even a final season, when the previous seasons or acts succeeded without that weight. So overall a great film and I have really been grateful for some good films this run. Hence a 10/10 on my ridiculously skewed upward movie rating scale.
